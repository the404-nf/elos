# Icon style guide

How every Resonant icon is drawn, so a new one (drawn by a person or generated by an agent) matches the 119 already in the set. Read it before drawing, check against it before merging. The set itself lives in `assets/icons/` and on the Icon contract (`ds.icon`). The decisions behind these rules, with their context and the options weighed, are recorded as DDR-0001 to DDR-0008 in `docs/decisions/icons.md`.

## The decisions, and why

| Decision | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Grid | 24 by 24 units, live area 2 to 22 | One grid for every size; 2 units of padding keep icons optically even. |
| Stroke | 2 units, one weight per icon | Approved after comparing 1.75, 2, 2.25 and 2.5. Reads clearly at 16px, does not get heavy at 32px. |
| Scaling | Stroke in grid units, never pixels | The stroke grows with the icon: 1px at 12, 1.33 at 16, 1.67 at 20, 2 at 24, 2.67 at 32. No per-size redraws. |
| Sizes | `icon-size-xs` 12, `sm` 16, `md` 20, `lg` 24, `xl` 32 | Five steps. md is the default. |
| Caps and joins | Round | Matches the pill buttons and soft radii. No square ends anywhere. |
| Closed shapes | 3-unit corners | The icon version of `radius-container`. |
| Small inner parts | Pills (fully round ends) | Doors, rows, handles, bars. Matches `radius-control`. |
| Air | At least 1 unit between any two strokes | Centerlines 3 units apart at stroke 2. Keeps 12px icons legible. |
| Color | `currentColor` | Icons take the text color, ink or white. |
| Accent | At most one part, in `--icon-accent` | The only place pink lives inside an icon. |
| Accent rule | Accent parts float | They never cross, touch or end on a mono stroke. Pink and white never meet. Decided on the home icon. |
| Fills | Only small marks | Dots, and caps like the pencil's eraser that would close up if stroked. No solid icon variants. |
| Source | Drawn here | No third-party set, no emoji. |

## Grid and keylines

Draw on the 24 grid with these keyline shapes as the starting proportions:

| Keyline | Size | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Circle | 18 across (r 9, center 12 12) | Status icons, publish, globe, target |
| Square | 16 by 16, corner 3 | Dashboard, columns, padding, opacity |
| Portrait | 14 by 18, corner 3 | Page, diff, tablet |
| Landscape | 18 by 14, corner 3 | Terminal, image, mail, window |

Optical sizes: a circle may go to 18 across; squares stay at 16 to 17 so they don't look bigger than circles. Diagonal subjects (edit, eyedropper) are drawn level and rotated 45 degrees around the center.

## The accent

- One accent part per icon at most. A group of dots or a pair of knobs counts as one part.
- The accent marks the point of the icon: the door of home, the eraser of edit, the knobs of settings, the drop of fill, the minus or plus of zoom.
- Accent parts keep the same 1 unit of air from mono strokes as any two strokes do. If a part has to connect to the outline, it stays mono.
- Where a guide must pass through objects (the align icons), the icon stays fully mono. Distribute icons use the accent on the free-standing middle object.
- In the SVG, mark the accent with `class="rs-i-a"` (stroked) or `class="rs-i-f rs-i-a"` (filled). Standalone files use `style="stroke:var(--icon-accent,currentColor)"` so they fall back to mono.

### Examples from the approval rounds

| Icon | Rejected | Approved |
|---|---|---|
| home | Pink door legs crossing the white floor | Open floor: walls curve into the floor and stop, the pink door stands in the gap |
| home (alternative) | Pink floor segment under the door | Rejected because pink and white still meet end to end, which leaves a seam at small sizes |
| edit | Pink band crossing the pencil body | Pink eraser cap floating just off the body |
| settings | Pink knobs sitting on the white rails | Each rail stops short of its knob |
| chart-line | Pink arrowhead touching the line | Fully mono |

## Size checks

Every icon must read at 16px. These need sm (16) or larger and must not be used at 12: `keyboard`, `command`, `markdown`, `calendar`, `chat`, `gradient`, `opacity`, `palette`, `hand`. A new icon that only works at 20 and up goes on this list.

## File format

```svg
<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24"
     fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2"
     stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" aria-hidden="true">
  <!-- mono parts: plain paths, rects and circles -->
  <!-- accent part: style="stroke:var(--icon-accent,currentColor)" -->
  <!-- filled accent: style="fill:var(--icon-accent,currentColor);stroke:none" -->
</svg>
```

- File name is the icon name, kebab-case, a noun or the action: `zoom-in.svg`, `align-left.svg`.
- Coordinates on the grid, with halves allowed (`10.5`). Keep paths short; no transforms except the 45 degree rotation for diagonal subjects.
- No `vector-effect="non-scaling-stroke"`, no filters, no gradients, no text.

## Adding an icon

1. Check the set first. Reuse before drawing; `chevron-down` rotated is not a new icon.
2. Pick the keyline that fits the subject and draw mono first.
3. Decide whether it needs an accent. Most do not. If it does, pick the one part that is the point, and float it.
4. Check air: every pair of strokes at least 3 units apart centerline to centerline, accent included.
5. Render at 12, 16, 20, 24 and 32 in dark and light, mono and accent, next to five existing icons from the same group.
6. Add it to its group in `assets/icons/`, the Icon contract (`ds.icon`) and the `name` enum of `ds.icon`.

## Review checklist

- [ ] 24 grid, nothing outside 2 to 22
- [ ] One stroke weight, 2 units, round caps and joins
- [ ] Closed shapes have 3-unit corners, inner parts are pills
- [ ] At least 1 unit of air between any two strokes
- [ ] Zero or one accent part, floating
- [ ] Reads at 16px; listed if it needs 16 or more
- [ ] Matches the weight and density of its group
- [ ] `currentColor` only, accent through `--icon-accent`
- [ ] No text, no gradients, no third-party shapes or brand marks

## Brief for an agent

Paste this when asking an agent to draw new icons:

> Draw a Resonant icon named `<name>` for `<what it means>`. 24 by 24 viewBox, live area 2 to 22. One stroke, 2 units, `stroke="currentColor"`, round caps and joins, `fill="none"`. Closed shapes take 3-unit corners; small inner parts are pills. Keep at least 3 units between stroke centerlines. Use at most one accent part, the one that carries the meaning, styled `stroke:var(--icon-accent,currentColor)` (or fill for small dots), and keep it from touching or crossing any other stroke. Fills only for small dots. No text, gradients, filters or transforms other than a 45 degree rotation around 12 12. It must read at 16px. Return the SVG and one sentence on the accent choice.
